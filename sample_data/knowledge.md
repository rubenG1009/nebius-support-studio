# Northstar Shop — sample policy (fictional)

## Shipping
Standard delivery within mainland Spain takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Orders above €50 receive free standard shipping. Express delivery is not offered in this demo.

## Returns
Customers can request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is received and checked.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, the customer should contact support with an order number and a photo of the damage. Support reviews the evidence before offering a replacement or refund.

## Order changes
An order can be changed or cancelled before dispatch. After dispatch, the customer must use the returns process. Support cannot promise a cancellation without checking the order status.
